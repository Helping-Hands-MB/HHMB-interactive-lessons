## 2. Magnetic Poles
Every magnet has two ends, called poles. There is a North pole (N) and a South pole (S).

In the simulator, you will see a bar magnet. Try moving the magnet around.

Opposite poles attract each other (North and South), while the same poles repel each other (North and North, or South and South).

Continue by clicking the "Next Step" button.

## 3. Magnetic Fields
A magnetic field is the invisible area around a magnet where its magnetic force can be felt.

You can use a compass to see the direction of the magnetic field. Notice how the compass needle changes direction as you move it around the magnet. The red end of the compass always points towards the South pole of the magnet, and the white end points towards the North pole.

## 4. Electromagnets
Did you know that electricity can create magnetism? When electricity flows through a wire, it creates a small magnetic field.

If you wrap the wire into a coil, the magnetic field becomes stronger. This is called an electromagnet!

In the simulator, try moving the magnet through the coil of wire. What happens to the light bulb?

**Question**: Does moving the magnet faster or slower change how bright the light bulb gets?

## 5. Faraday's Law
When you move a magnet through a coil of wire, it creates an electrical current. This is called Faraday's Law of Induction.

**Try it yourself**: What happens if you add more loops to the coil of wire? Does the light bulb get brighter or dimmer when you move the magnet through it?
