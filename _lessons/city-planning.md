## 1. Welcome & City Limits
Welcome, future urban designers and architects! Welcome to the Helping Hands MB City Planning Workshop. Today, you are going to learn how real planners design communities where people can live, work, and play together safely and happily.

[Urban Planning 101](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=98GwUdouZi0)

## 2. Neighborhoods
Neighborhoods are residential zones where people live. Grab the 'Neighborhood' tool from the left menu and draw a block of houses.

**Question**: Where would you prefer to live: near the center of town or near the edge?

## 3. Businesses and Shops
Commercial zones provide places to work and shop. Grab the 'Business' tool and place some shops near your neighborhood so citizens don't have to travel far.

**Question**: Why is it important to have businesses close to neighborhoods?

## 4. Parks and Recreation
Green spaces are crucial for clean air and recreation. Use the 'Park' tool to draw a park next to the neighborhood.

## 5. Transportation
Connect your zones! Use the 'Road' or 'Walkway' tools to connect the houses to the shops and the park.

## 6. Public Services
A city needs more than just houses and shops to thrive. It needs schools for education, hospitals for health, and fire stations for safety.

**Question**: Where is the best place to put a fire station so that fire engines can reach all parts of the city quickly? Try placing one on your map.

## 7. Sustainability and Future Planning
Great cities plan for the future! Sustainability means building in a way that doesn't harm the environment, like using renewable energy or designing good public transit so people don't need cars as much.

**Question**: How can you make your city more sustainable? Think about where you placed your parks and how easy it is to walk from the neighborhood to the businesses.
